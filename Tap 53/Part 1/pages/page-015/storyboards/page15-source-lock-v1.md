# Page 15 — source lock v1

## Confirmed story state

- Blue hour immediately after Visakha and Punna leave for Savatthi.
- Nalini has two obsolete plan rolls and has not started redrawing.
- The chapter-ending change is internal: he begins seeing the monastery as a
  place people will live in, not only as a builder's plan.
- Only Nalini needs to be visible.

## Worksite scale and geography

- Chapter 1 lines 15–16: a very large worksite with hundreds of people.
- Lines 57, 61 and 66–67: several spatial zones, abundant land, a far edge beside
  a windswept field and distant workers.
- Chapter 2 lines 16 and 19: ordered rows of stakes and dozens of large
  foundation holes.
- Chapter 2 line 31: surrounding fields read as vast.
- Visual terrain: open low-relief plain, long low horizon, optionally a sparse
  low tree line. No mountain, hill, ridge, cliff, valley wall, water body or
  dense enclosing forest.

## Temporary shelter class

- Chapter 1 line 24: worksite rest shelters already serve workers.
- Lines 83–85: more leaf shelters can be erected quickly for rest and shade.
- Line 87: torchlight flickers inside them at blue hour.
- Chapter 2 lines 3–5: a shelter can be long enough for a seated row while still
  remaining temporary.
- Visual class: long low canopy made from thin irregular roundwood poles set in
  earth, simple visible lashings, shallow improvised rafters and loose dry-leaf
  bundles with uneven edges; fully open sides and bare earth floor.
- Forbidden: finished pavilion, thick dressed columns, bases/capitals, plinth,
  monumental or multi-tier roof, finished joinery, masonry, walls, raised floor,
  railing, paving or built furniture.

## Separation from Chapter 3

Chapter 3's dense forest and ancient stone temple are a different location.
None of their stone, ruin, enclosure or forest language may appear on page 15.

## Emotional priority

Nalini's changed perception is the focus. The vast worksite and humble shelters
are evidence for that change, not an architectural showcase. Technical locks
must remain invisible in the final image.
