# Prompt — page10-restart-optionA — H1 v1

Use case: illustration-story / historical-scene
Asset type: one near-square raster storyboard shot for the oval image frame on
Tập 53 Part 1 page 10; compose for a 1.10:1 usable image, not for a full spread.

Input images:
- Image 1: character/profile reference for Bác Nalini; preserve his face,
  orange-gold turban with bead band, long dark curls, moustache/short beard,
  cream shirt, saffron drape and brown-gold dhoti.
- Image 2: character/profile reference for Visakha; preserve her face, green
  sari and veil with gold trim, blue blouse, gold jewelry and dignified modest
  silhouette.

Primary request: a single quiet medium close-up two-shot at an ancient Indian
construction-material staging area in the afternoon. This is the exact moment
when Visakha has just seen extra heavy blue-green stone blocks and gently asks
Nalini to use them for the Buddha's sacred residence. Nalini is still before
his spoken agreement, but his practical calculation is beginning to soften into
respect.

Scene/backdrop: rough open construction ground, a few heavy blue-green stone
blocks, subtle partial ox-cart load of ordinary fired bricks not yet unloaded,
and at most one or two tiny indistinct workers moving stone in the far
background. The site is still under construction; keep the background simple
and subordinate.

Composition/framing: near-square 1.10:1 image. Nalini is a readable bust/medium
close-up in the left half, holding a rolled blueprint low and turning toward
the shared stone focus. Visakha is a readable bust/medium close-up in the right
half. Her open palm, with softly grouped fingers, gestures toward the stone;
it must not become a single pointing finger or a commanding gesture. Place the
stone blocks low and to the sides, never as a critical object in the center
spine strip. Leave a quiet, low-contrast strip of ground between the two
characters for the book gutter. Eye path: Visakha's softened eyes and open
palm -> blue-green stone -> Nalini's softened listening eyes.

Emotion: Visakha is sincere, tender and non-showy; Nalini is attentive,
respectful and just beginning to understand. Nalini's eyes are soft, eyebrows
released, mouth lightly closed; his torso leans subtly toward the shared focus.
Visakha's shoulders and elbow are relaxed.

Style/medium: pure 2D animation, crisp consistent line art, flat color fills,
one or two hard cel-shadows, graphic ancient-India character design. Background
uses the same simplified 2D line language.

Constraints: exactly two clearly visible characters, correct profile identity
and clothing, no face/hand/blueprint/stone touching the center gutter area, no
full-body or wide establishing composition, no text, no watermark.

Background contract: show only the early construction-material staging phase:
rough ground, extra blue-green stone blocks, and optional unloaded brick cart.
Do not show a completed shrine, finished wall, columns, roof, interior, monks or
nuns, worship scene, later construction stage, or a clean finished compound.

Avoid: Punna as a visible focus, crowds, extra named characters, workers in the
foreground, modern objects, Chinese/East Asian clothing, fantasy architecture,
temple completion, direct frontal posing, both characters staring at the viewer,
argument, suspicion, cost-calculation grimace, crossed arms, chin-resting,
pointing finger, clenched fist, exaggerated smile, full body, distant faces,
gutter obstruction, CGI, 3D, 2.5D, photorealism, soft airbrush, heavy gradients,
blur, bloom, bokeh, lens flare, volumetric light.
