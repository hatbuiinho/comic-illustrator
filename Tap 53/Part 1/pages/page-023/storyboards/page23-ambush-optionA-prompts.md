# Page 23 — Ambush option A prompts v1

## H1 — suspended strike

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: comic storyboard shot, standalone raster for the left near-square frame
Primary request: A tense pre-impact ambush on a wild forest path. Camera is low and directly over the left shoulder of the ascetic attacker. The ascetic is in the foreground left, lunging forward with a raised wooden club descending diagonally toward the back of the bald monk ahead. The club is clearly close but has not touched him. The monk is walking calmly away, seen from behind and slightly in profile; he does not turn, look back, defend himself, or know about the danger.
Input images: Image 1 is the ascetic character profile reference; Image 2 is the Moggallana character profile reference.
Scene/backdrop: sparse wild forest path, dirt trail, natural rocks and trees, no architecture.
Subject: only two visible characters, the ascetic attacker and Ngài Mục Kiền Liên.
Style/medium: pure 2D animation, crisp clean line art, flat color blocks, 1–2 hard cel-shading levels, graphic shapes; no 3D or realistic rendering.
Composition/framing: near-square composition; attacker shoulder, gripping hands, face and club occupy the left half; monk's back, bald head and shoulder occupy the right half; keep all faces, hands and club outside the center gutter; medium-wide over-the-shoulder shot.
Lighting/mood: clear readable daylight, tense suspended instant, restrained contrast.
Color palette: earthy browns, muted forest greens, warm skin tones.
Materials/textures: simplified 2D painted forest, flat fabric shapes, no photoreal texture.
Text (verbatim): none.
Constraints: preserve the ascetic's high bun, long hair, moustache, prayer beads and torn brown robe; preserve the monk's bald head and brown robe. Show the attack intention and asymmetry of knowledge. The club must stop short of contact.
Avoid: impact, injury, blood, monk looking back, monk reacting, face-to-face confrontation, ravine, cliff, shrine, magic, aura, glow, volumetric light, bloom, blur, bokeh, lens flare, CGI, 2.5D, soft airbrush, gradient-heavy shading, extra characters, text, watermark.

## H2 — ravine wake

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: comic storyboard shot, standalone raster for the right near-square frame
Primary request: The same ascetic has just awakened alone at the bottom of a deep remote ravine. Camera is very low beside the rock floor, three-quarter front-left, medium close shot. He is pushing himself up with one hand while the other hand firmly clutches his left side; his face is dazed, disoriented and alarmed. Tall rock walls dominate the frame and a very small distant patch of sky appears high at the upper right.
Input images: Image 1 is the ascetic character profile reference.
Scene/backdrop: barren ravine floor, angular natural rocks, towering cliff walls, tiny distant sky opening.
Subject: only the ascetic, no other person.
Style/medium: pure 2D animation, crisp clean line art, flat color blocks, 1–2 hard cel-shading levels, graphic shapes; no 3D or realistic rendering.
Composition/framing: near-square composition; face and clutching hand are large and readable in the lower-left half; rock walls compress the frame; tiny sky opening at upper right; no visible ravine mouth.
Lighting/mood: hard restrained daylight from the distant opening, disorientation and isolation.
Color palette: dark slate rock, muted earth brown robe, small cool blue sky, warm skin tones.
Materials/textures: simplified 2D rock planes and flat robe folds, no photoreal texture.
Text (verbatim): none.
Constraints: preserve the ascetic's high bun, long hair, moustache, prayer beads and torn brown robe. Make the left-side clutching gesture unmistakable.
Avoid: Ngài Mục Kiền Liên, club, forest path, shrine, ravine mouth, visible attacker context, magic, aura, glow, volumetric light, bloom, blur, bokeh, lens flare, CGI, 2.5D, soft airbrush, gradient-heavy shading, extra characters, text, watermark.
