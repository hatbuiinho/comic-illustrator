# Page 24 — Option A prompts

## H1 — fist silences the circle

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: comic storyboard raster, horizontal
strip, target ratio 2.57:1.

Interior of an old stone shrine hidden in a forest, viewed from inside the
small seated circle. Low medium-wide composition in a narrow horizontal strip.
Exactly five ascetics are visible: an elderly Taoist leader with white hair and
white beard in a high bun and orange head cloth at left-center; a thin anxious
young ascetic; a bearded ascetic rubbing his left side; a large tall ascetic;
and an ascetic seated near the cracked doorway. The last proposal has just been
made. In the exact moment after it, the elder clenches one fist and the whole
circle falls silent. The fist is the largest foreground anchor, then the elder's
face, then four distinct silent supporting profiles, then a very small oil lamp
that is still burning.

Place fist at left foreground, elder face just behind it, supporting figures in
compressed depth toward the right, and keep the right edge as simplified dark
stone before the spine. Preserve readable silhouettes and at least two profile
identifiers for each supporting ascetic. Keep faces, fist, lamp and all critical
story details away from the spine and text reserve. The oil lamp flame is tiny
and local, not the focus.

Pure 2D animation throughout every component: clean consistent dark line art,
flat earth-tone color blocks, hard-edged 1–2 level cel shading, simplified
angular stone shapes, graphic facial features, graphic fabric and beard shapes.
The background, stone, lamp and light must use the same 2D language as the
characters. No photoreal texture, realistic stone, 3D, 2.5D, CGI, soft
airbrush, heavy gradients, bloom, volumetric light, god rays, lens flare,
depth of field, bokeh, blur, weapons, women, Buddhist figures, palace,
monastery, sixth character, future action, speaking, arguing or visible plan.
No text, lettering or watermark.

## H2 — extinguished lamp

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: comic storyboard raster, horizontal
strip, target ratio 2.57:1.

Extreme close-up horizontal strip inside the same old cracked stone shrine,
immediately after a draft through the cracked wall extinguishes the oil lamp.
No characters. Show a small earthenware oil lamp with a completely black wick;
there is no remaining flame. A thin graphic smoke ribbon moves horizontally from
the wick toward a visible crack in the stone wall. Read in this order: black
wick, horizontal smoke, cracked wall, darkness. Keep the left edge and the area
near the spine as simplified dark stone and preserve clear breathing room.

Pure 2D animation throughout: clear uniform line art, flat color blocks,
hard-edged limited cel shading, angular simplified cracked stone, graphic
1–2-band smoke. No photoreal ceramic or stone texture, no realistic smoke, no
soft haze, no blur, no bloom, no gradient-heavy lighting, no 3D or CGI, no
volumetric light, no people, no flame, no insects, no text or watermark.

## Reprocess v2 — identity blocks bắt buộc

Giữ nguyên bố cục H1 Option A, nhưng ưu tiên identity fidelity trước chi tiết
nền. Dùng 5 profile reference đã xem trong manifest. Không thêm tượng, phù điêu,
cửa mở hoặc rừng ngoài; nền là tường đá phẳng, tối, góc cạnh.

- `lao-dao-truong`: tóc/râu trắng dài, búi trắng cao, mặt già góc cạnh, khăn
  cam, chuỗi hạt nâu; mặt 3/4 lớn bên trái và nắm tay foreground.
- `dao-si-tre-gay`: thân rất gầy, tóc đen búi cao có tóc dài sau gáy, ria mép và
  chòm râu nhỏ, mắt to lo âu, áo nâu-be với dải đỏ nâu; ở sau lão.
- `ga-xoa-man-suon`: tóc đen búi cao, ria mép đen dày nhưng không râu dài, thân
  trung bình, áo nâu cam, tay trái ôm/xoa mạn sườn; ở giữa.
- `ga-to-cao`: cao và cơ bắp lớn nhất nhóm, tóc đen búi cao, ria cong dày,
  chòm râu nhọn, khăn đỏ nâu; nền phải.
- `dao-si-gan-cua`: tóc nâu đen búi cao, râu quai nón, dải vải kem chéo ngực,
  áo nâu; sát mảng tường nứt ở mép phải.

Không hoán đổi các bộ nhận dạng trên. Mỗi nhân vật phải giữ đúng mặt, tóc/râu,
vóc dáng, màu vải và silhouette của profile riêng. Background chỉ là nội thất
đá 2D phẳng cùng ngôn ngữ nét và cel-shading với nhân vật; không có Buddhist
relief/statue, không có ngoại cảnh, không có moss/texture ảnh thật.
